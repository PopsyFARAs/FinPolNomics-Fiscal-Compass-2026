    Module 03 · Act No. 7 of 2025 · commencement 1 January 2026

    Nigeria Tax Act 2025

    The charging statute. It consolidates income tax, capital gains, VAT, stamp duties and the petroleum fiscal chapter into one Act, and repeals the legacy statutes listed in s.195.

    Module 04 · Act No. 5 of 2025

    Nigeria Tax Administration Act 2025

    The procedural statute: registration, returns, payment, audit, assessment, objection, refunds, penalties and the limits of rule-making.

    Module 06 · NTA 2025 Chapter Three, Sixth and Seventh Schedules · PIA 2021 (non-fiscal parts)

    Petroleum and gas fiscal regime

    NTA 2025 s.196(1) deleted PIA 2021 Chapter Four Parts I to X. Hydrocarbon Tax, petroleum CIT and royalties now live in the NTA. Licensing, host communities, funds and regulators remain in the PIA.
